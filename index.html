Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shadow Dodge ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles information when you download, install, and play our mobile application.

1. Summary & Core Principle

Shadow Dodge is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not require account registration, do not collect personal identifiers (such as your name, email, or physical address), and the core game runs entirely offline on your device.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All gameplay data is saved strictly on your local device storage using standard browser/webview local storage. This includes:

  • High scores and survival times
  • Stardust balance and unlocked cosmetics (skins and trails)
  • Achievement progression and daily challenge streaks
  • Sound and control preference settings

This data never leaves your device unless you clear the application cache or uninstall the app.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Shadow Dodge may display third-party advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support ongoing development.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal and technical device information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, which may include:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device model, operating system version, and screen specifications
  • Coarse network data (IP address, country level) and diagnostic data for ad performance and fraud prevention

For more details on how Google processes data, please visit Google Advertising & Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy

Shadow Dodge does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has inadvertently been collected, please contact us immediately so we can take necessary actions.

5. In-App Purchases (Optional)

Any optional in-app purchases (such as cosmetic items or ad removal) are processed securely through the official Google Play Billing system. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card details or financial information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
